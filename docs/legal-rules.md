# Legal rules for everything we publish (site, videos, ads, social posts)

Read before publishing anything. These exist to keep us out of trouble.
This is a practical checklist, not legal advice.

## 1. Assets (logos, images, music, fonts)

- **Never** take an image, logo, icon, or font from Google Images or a random website.
- Every asset must be: made by us, paid for, or from a source with a written
  free license — and keep the link or receipt proving it.
- **Logo:** the current avatar (`site/assets/avatar.png`) is ours (provided by
  the owner). When making new marks, do not imitate famous brands
  (e.g. the Vercel triangle, the Venmo "V", the Visa wordmark).
- **Music in videos:**
  - Trending sounds are licensed ONLY for personal/organic posts — **never for
    ads or branded content**.
  - For ads / branded / business videos use royalty-free music
    (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, Uppbeat) or the platform's commercial
    music library. Save proof of the license.
- **Fonts:** keep using system fonts on the site. Any custom font needs a
  license file added next to it.

## 2. Claims (site, posts, videos)

- No fake "As seen in CNN / Forbes / ..." unless we can link the real article.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, customer logos, or made-up numbers.
- No "guaranteed results". No medical, financial, or legal advice claims.
- Any statistic we quote must be true and sourced at the moment we use it.

## 3. Code

- Only original code, or MIT / Apache-2.0 / BSD with attribution added to
  `THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- Never paste GPL / AGPL code into this repository.

## 4. Data

- Free tool: runs in the browser; logs never leave the machine. Say exactly that.
- Paid clients: we receive only the logs they choose to send; delete on request;
  never resell. Put it on the site (see `site/privacy.html`).

## 5. Payments

- Money goes through a licensed Merchant of Record — it handles taxes and
  receipts for every country.
- Refunds: monthly plans can cancel anytime; yearly at renewal; month one is
  credited if the first report finds nothing actionable.
