FAQ

Is my data safe?

The free tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored. For paid plans we handle only the logs you choose to share, delete them on request (no later than 30 days after your subscription ends), and never resell them. See Security.

What formats do you support?

JSONL, JSON arrays, raw text. OpenAI, Anthropic, LangChain, Claude Code, custom frameworks — the parser is tolerant, and exotic formats are adapted quickly.

How fast do I get results?

Free autopsy: instant. First watchtower report: within 7 days of connecting logs. Alerts: minutes. Command auto-pause: seconds.

What if you find nothing?

Month one is credited if the first report finds nothing actionable. If your logs are clean, we say so, in writing.

How does the watchtower install?

One command in your environment. Read-only, scoped credentials, kill-switch documented, complete rollback. Security details: Security.
