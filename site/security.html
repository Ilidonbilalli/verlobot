Security

We watch your agents. Here's how we make sure we're never the risk.

Read-only, always

The watchtower only reads logs. It never writes to your systems, repos, or customer data. Auto-pause (Command) only pauses an agent you flag — it never takes other actions on your infrastructure.

Scoped tokens

Connect with a token that can do exactly one thing: submit logs. It cannot list your files, call your APIs, or touch anything else. Revoke anytime in one click.

Logs can stay home

The free autopsy never uploads anything. On paid plans, the default is analysis on your machine; only the summaries and the leak report ever travel.

Kill switch & rollback

One command disconnects everything. Every change we make ships as a reviewable pull request with evidence — nothing direct, nothing silent.

Deletion on request

We delete your logs within 30 days of your subscription ending, or immediately on request. No resale. No training on your data. In writing.

NDA on request

Enterprise teams can onboard under NDA before sharing logs. Standard engagements include a data-processing addendum.

Controls we follow

We build to the same bar public frameworks set: OWASP ASVS (L2/L3), least-privilege scoping, audit logging of every action we take, and WCAG 2.2 accessibility on this site.

  • Scoped, revocable credentials — no broad access
  • Every action we take is logged and attributable
  • No secrets on disk; no cross-tenant access
  • Data minimization — we use what you send, nothing more
