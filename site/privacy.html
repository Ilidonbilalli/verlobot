Privacy Policy

Short version: the free tool never sees your logs. On paid plans, we only handle what you choose to send us.

Free autopsy tool

Runs entirely in your browser. Your log file is read locally by the page's own code and is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted. Close the tab — everything is gone. You can verify this with your browser's developer tools.

What we collect on paid plans

Only the logs or repository access you choose to share, plus the contact details you give us (name, work email, company). We use them for one purpose: finding and fixing reliability failures for you.

Purposes & sharing

We never resell your data. We never train models on it. We share it only with the payment processor (as required to bill you) and, on request, under NDA.

Payments

Card payments are processed by a licensed Merchant of Record, which acts as the seller for tax purposes worldwide. Your card details go to them, not to us. Their privacy policy applies to payment data. We receive only payment status, product, and amount.

Retention & deletion

Your logs are deleted no later than 30 days after your subscription ends, or immediately on request.
