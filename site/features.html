Features

Everything below ships. Ordered from free to Command-only. If you want any of it for a 20-minute call, bring your logs.

Agent Autopsy (free)

Browser-based leak scan across 8 failure classes, with exact-turn evidence. Nothing leaves the machine.

Instant loop alerts

Identical action repeated 3× or an error storm → you get Slack/email within minutes.

Regression guard

A leak that was fixed last month and came back is flagged immediately — with the diff in behavior.

Monthly reliability report

Score, what broke, what it cost, what we fixed, and what's next — exportable evidence.

Top-fixes included

Trend history

Watch scores rise month over month so your leadership can see reliability compounding.
